01 / How It Works

Understanding LeadsPitch

What LeadsPitch is, how it works, and who it's built for.

What is LeadsPitch?

LeadsPitch is a business data platform. We deliver verified, industry-specific business datasets as one-time purchases. No software to learn, no credits to manage.
You choose an industry and a plan on our Bundles page. Buy a single dataset and download it instantly. There's no software to log into or dashboard to search.
LeadsPitch is built for agency owners running outbound for clients, founders doing their own outreach, freelance recruiters, and small sales teams who want reliable business data without operating a full prospecting platform.
Apollo and ZoomInfo are software platforms with searchable dashboards and credit-based pricing. LeadsPitch doesn't require you to search for data or manage credits. You receive a finished dataset on a schedule instead of building one yourself inside a tool.
Business data goes stale quickly. People change roles, companies close, and contact details age. A subscription means you get a fresh dataset on a regular schedule. If you only need data once, a one-time purchase is also available.

03 / One-Time Purchases

One-Time Datasets

How one-time purchases work and how they differ from subscriptions.

A one-time purchase gives you a single dataset for a specific industry. It's a one-shot delivery with no recurring billing. If you need fresh data later, you can purchase again.
A subscription delivers a new dataset every billing period. A one-time purchase delivers a single dataset. Subscriptions include automatic renewals; one-time purchases don't.

04 / Datasets & Quality

Datasets & Data Quality

What's included in a dataset, how we verify records, and where the data comes from.

Depending on the industry and plan, a dataset may include verified business email addresses, phone numbers, company name, owner or decision-maker name, job title, website, city, state, country, industry classification, company size, and LinkedIn company profile, where available. Not every field is available for every record.

What does "verified" mean?

It means each record is checked before delivery to confirm the information is accurate and current. Where a specific detail can't be reasonably confirmed, we leave that field blank rather than include something we're not confident about.

How often is data refreshed?

Every dataset is verified before delivery to ensure accuracy. You receive a fresh, clean CSV file ready for outreach.
We verify records before delivery, but no business dataset is ever 100% complete or error-free. Contact details change constantly. If you find a record that's inaccurate, let us know so we can look into it.
LeadsPitch datasets are compiled from publicly available and licensed sources related to business and professional activity. Each industry dataset is built specifically for that industry rather than filtered from one large, general database. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
LeadsPitch currently offers datasets across 19+ industries including Healthcare, Legal, Real Estate, Ecommerce, SaaS, B2B Companies, and more. Each dataset is built specifically for its industry. See the Niches page for details on each.
We expect to add industries over time. We'll announce new industries when they're actually available rather than promising a specific timeline in advance.

07 / Refunds

Refund Policy

When refunds apply, how to request one, and what to expect.

What is the refund policy?

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a dataset has been delivered for that billing period. One-time purchases follow the same policy. Exceptions apply for duplicate charges, billing errors, or failed deliveries. Full details are in our Refund Policy.
If you don't receive your dataset for a billing period you were charged for, contact us. We'll investigate and either redeliver the dataset or issue a refund for that period.
Use our Contact page with your account details and a description of the issue. We'll review your request individually.

08 / Privacy

Privacy & Compliance

Legality, data handling, GDPR, CCPA, and how to request removal.

Is buying business data legal?

Yes, buying business data is legal in most jurisdictions when the provider sources and handles data responsibly. It's your responsibility to make sure how you use that data, including outreach and marketing, complies with laws that apply to you, such as CAN-SPAM, GDPR, or CCPA depending on where you and your recipients are located.
We describe how we collect, use, and share information in our Privacy Policy, including sections specific to GDPR and CCPA. If you have rights under either regulation, you can exercise them by contacting us directly.
Anyone whose business contact information appears in a LeadsPitch dataset can request removal by contacting us. We apply approved removal requests to future datasets going forward.
Information about you as a website visitor or customer, like your name, email, and billing details, is handled separately from the business datasets we sell. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
