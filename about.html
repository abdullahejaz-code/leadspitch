Business data, delivered without the platform.

LeadsPitch is a trusted lead data provider and verified business owner database. We deliver premium, industry-specific lead lists as ready-to-use datasets, through monthly subscriptions or one-time purchases, with no software to learn and no credits to manage.

Getting current business data shouldn't require learning a new piece of software.

Most companies that need business contact data aren't trying to become power users of a sales intelligence platform. They just need accurate information about a specific industry, on a schedule they can rely on, at a price they can plan around.

LeadsPitch exists to do exactly that and nothing more complicated.

Why LeadsPitch Exists

The market for business data has mostly been built around one model.

A searchable database wrapped in software. You log in, you search, you spend credits, you export what you find. That model works well for teams that want to build and manage their own lists inside a platform.

It doesn't work as well for everyone else. Agencies juggling multiple client accounts. Founders doing their own outreach with no sales team behind them. Recruiters who need one industry's worth of data, not access to everything. Small sales teams who don't want to pay per seat for software built with enterprise workflows in mind.

LeadsPitch was built for that second group. Instead of asking you to operate a platform, we deliver a finished dataset on a schedule.

Three things tend to frustrate people who try existing sales intelligence platforms:

  • 01

    The pricing is unpredictable.

    Credit systems mean your cost changes based on how much you use the tool, which makes budgeting harder than it should be.

  • 02

    The software is more than the job requires.

    Dashboards, filters, integrations, and browser extensions are useful if you're running a large-scale outbound operation. If you just need this month's data for one industry, all of that is overhead.

  • 03

    The data goes stale.

    A one-time list purchase is accurate on the day you buy it and less accurate every day after. Contacts change roles, companies close, information ages.

LeadsPitch is built for people who need reliable business data but don't want to run a prospecting platform to get it.

  • Agency owners running outbound for multiple clients
  • Founders handling their own outreach without a dedicated sales team
  • Freelance and small-shop recruiters building industry-specific pipelines
  • Small sales teams who need reliable data without enterprise software

What Guides How We Operate

Our Principles

01

Say what's true, not what sounds good.

We'd rather tell you plainly what a dataset includes, and doesn't include, than round up. If a field can't be verified, we leave it blank instead of guessing.

02

Simple by design, not by limitation.

LeadsPitch doesn't have a dashboard because we chose not to build one, not because we couldn't. Delivery is the product, not a smaller version of one.

03

Data you can question.

You should be able to ask how a dataset was built and get a real answer. If something about our sourcing or verification process doesn't make sense to you, we want to hear about it.

How We Handle Data Quality and Transparency

Our Approach to Business Data

Sourcing

Built for one industry at a time.

Each industry dataset is built specifically for that industry, rather than filtered out of one large, general-purpose database. We source and compile business data with that specificity in mind, so the fields you receive are relevant to the industry you've chosen.

Verification

Verified before delivery.

Before a dataset is delivered, we work to verify the records in it. Where a piece of information can't be reasonably confirmed, we leave the field empty rather than include something we're not confident about.

Opt-Outs

Removal on request.

If your business contact information appears in a LeadsPitch dataset and you'd like it removed, you can request that directly. We apply removal requests to future datasets going forward.

Where LeadsPitch Is Headed

We're not going to describe features that don't exist yet or promise a roadmap we haven't built.

Today, LeadsPitch's core offerings are industry datasets available through subscriptions and one-time purchases. That's where we've focused, and it's what we'd recommend judging us on right now.

As a business data platform, we expect the ways we deliver business data to expand over time. When something new is ready, we'll explain it plainly, the same way we've tried to explain everything else on this page.

We're not trying to out-build Apollo or ZoomInfo on features. We're solving a narrower problem for people who never wanted to operate a full platform in the first place.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LeadsPitch?
LeadsPitch is a business data platform. We deliver verified industry datasets through monthly subscriptions or one-time purchases, with no software to learn and no credits to manage.
How is LeadsPitch different from Apollo or ZoomInfo?
Apollo and ZoomInfo are searchable software platforms with credit systems and dashboards. LeadsPitch doesn't require you to search or manage credits. You receive a finished dataset, either on a recurring schedule or as a one-time purchase, without operating any software.
How does LeadsPitch verify its data?
Each dataset is sourced and verified before delivery. Where a field can't be reasonably confirmed, we leave it blank rather than include unverified information.
Can I request removal of my business information?
Yes. If your business contact information appears in a LeadsPitch dataset and you'd like it removed, you can request that directly. We apply removal requests to future datasets going forward.
What industries does LeadsPitch cover?
LeadsPitch currently offers datasets across 19+ industries including Healthcare, Legal, Real Estate, Ecommerce, SaaS, B2B Companies, and more. Each dataset is built specifically for its industry. See the Industries page for details on each.
