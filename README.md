# LeadsPitch

Digital storefront for niche business lead lists. Static HTML/CSS site with a
Node.js generator (`generate-industries.js`) that produces the industry pages
in `industries/*.html` from a single data source.

## Pages

- `index.html` — home (pricing, industries, comparison, FAQ)
- `industries.html` — industry directory
- `industries/<slug>.html` — 21 industry pages (19 generated by
  `generate-industries.js`; `accounting-finance.html` and
  `travel-hospitality.html` are hand-maintained)
- `bundles.html` — multi-industry bundle pricing
- `about.html`, `faq.html`, `contact.html` — standard pages
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `refund.html` — legal pages (noindex)

## Develop

```bash
# Regenerate industry pages after editing generate-industries.js
node generate-industries.js

# Preview locally (e.g.)
npx serve .
```

## SEO

- Canonical URLs point at `https://leadspitch.com/...html`
- Open Graph / Twitter cards on all public pages
- JSON-LD: Organization, WebSite, Product, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage
- Qualitative comparison tables (no competitor pricing claims)
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` live at the site root
