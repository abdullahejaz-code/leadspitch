Overview

This Refund Policy explains how billing, cancellations, and refunds work for LeadsPitch subscription plans. It applies to our current subscription-based datasets and, where applicable, to future products and services we may offer.

One-Time Purchase Billing

LeadsPitch datasets are sold as one-time purchases. Payment is collected at the time of purchase and you receive your dataset immediately.

Refund Eligibility

Because each subscription period includes access to that month's delivered dataset, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a dataset has been delivered for that billing period.

If you believe you're entitled to a refund outside of the circumstances described below, contact us and we will review your request individually.

Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are not provided for:

  • Partial use of a dataset within a billing period
  • Dissatisfaction with the content of a dataset that was delivered as described for your plan
  • Failure to use or download a delivered dataset before canceling
  • Change of mind after a dataset has been delivered

Duplicate Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same billing period due to an error, contact us with your billing details. Verified duplicate charges will be refunded.

Failed Deliveries

If you do not receive your dataset for a billing period you were charged for, contact us. We will investigate and either redeliver the dataset or issue a refund for that billing period, depending on the circumstances.

Billing Errors

If you believe you were charged an incorrect amount or billed in error, contact us with details of the charge. We will review the billing record and correct verified errors, including issuing a refund where appropriate.

Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future billing but does not retroactively refund the current billing period, during which you retain access to that period's delivered dataset.

Automatic Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically at the start of each billing period unless canceled before the renewal date. It is your responsibility to cancel before renewal if you do not wish to be billed for the next period. We do not provide refunds for renewal charges made before a cancellation request is received.

Exceptional Refund Requests

We understand that circumstances vary. If you have a billing concern that isn't addressed elsewhere in this policy, contact us and describe the situation. We will review exceptional requests on a case-by-case basis.

Good to know

We're reasonable people. If something went wrong on our end, we'll make it right. Just reach out.

Contact Information

For billing questions, cancellations, or refund requests, contact us and we'll respond promptly.

Policy Updates

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
