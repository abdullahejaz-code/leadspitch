What pack sizes can I buy?

Packs start at 1,000 contacts ($7.99) and scale up through 5,000 ($29.99) and 12,000 ($59.99). The top Bulk & Bundles tier ($99.99+) has no row limit — ideal for whole-country pulls or multi-niche bundles. The bigger the pack, the lower the per-contact cost.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Apollo.io for small agencies?

Yes. Leadspitch is a one-time $7.99–$99.99 per pack instead of Apollo’s $49–$119 per user, per month. If you only need contacts for one niche or campaign, a single purchase beats even one month of a subscription.

Can I buy a lead list without a monthly subscription?

Yes. Every pack is a one-time purchase. You pay once, download the file, and there’s nothing to cancel later because nothing renews.

Is it legal to buy an email list and cold email people?

Yes — cold emailing business contacts is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM with a working unsubscribe link and accurate sender info. Our lists are built from publicly available business contact information for exactly this kind of B2B outreach. Follow CAN-SPAM (or GDPR for the EU/UK).

What file format do lead lists come in?

After you confirm checkout, your list is emailed to you as a ZIP containing an XLSX spreadsheet. We deliver it packaged in a ZIP by email — rather than a raw public link — to keep your data secure. The XLSX opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or any CRM and cold email tool that accepts spreadsheet imports.
