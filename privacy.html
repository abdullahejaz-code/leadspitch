Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LeadsPitch ("LeadsPitch," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with our website and our business data services.

LeadsPitch is a business data platform. Our core service delivers verified, industry-specific business datasets as one-time purchases. This policy covers both how we handle information about you as a visitor or customer, and how we handle business data included in the datasets we deliver.

By using our website or subscribing to our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account, subscribe to a plan, or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, business name, billing information, and any other details you choose to provide.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and general usage patterns. We collect this through standard web technologies, including cookies (see the Cookies section below).

Business Data and Publicly Available Information

Separate from information we collect about our website visitors and customers, LeadsPitch compiles and delivers business datasets as our core product. This business data may include names, job titles, business email addresses, business phone numbers, company information, and related professional details, sourced and verified for the industry categories we cover.

This business data is compiled from publicly available and licensed sources related to business and professional activity. It is intended for legitimate business purposes such as outreach, prospecting, and market research, and is provided to subscribers subject to the terms of our Terms of Service.

If your business information appears in a dataset: See the Opt-out and Removal Requests section below for how to request removal.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our subscription services
  • Process payments and manage billing
  • Deliver datasets to subscribers on the applicable schedule
  • Communicate with customers about their account or subscription
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet legal and compliance obligations

Important

We do not use personal information collected from website visitors or subscribers to build or supplement the business datasets we sell.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support core site functionality, remember preferences, and understand how our site is used. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand website traffic and usage patterns, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors navigate the site. Analytics data is generally aggregated and used to improve our website and services.

Marketing Communications

If you provide your email address to us, we may send you communications related to your account, subscription, or, where you have consented or as otherwise permitted by applicable law, marketing communications about LeadsPitch. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those messages or by contacting us directly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected from website visitors or customers to third parties.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our website, process payments, or deliver our services, under obligations to protect that information
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required by law
  • A successor entity in connection with a business transition such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy or an updated version communicated to you

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take steps to delete or anonymize it.

Security Measures

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Users

If you access our website or services from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be processed in a different jurisdiction. By using our services, you consent to this processing as described in this policy.

GDPR Considerations

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and the right to object to certain processing activities.

Where we process personal information on the basis of legitimate interest, including in connection with business contact data included in our datasets, you have the right to object to that processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly.

CCPA Considerations

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. LeadsPitch does not sell personal information collected from website visitors or customers. To exercise your rights under the CCPA, contact us directly.

Opt-out and Removal Requests

If your business contact information appears within a LeadsPitch dataset and you would like it removed, you may submit a removal request to us at the contact information below. We will apply approved removal requests to future datasets going forward. Removal requests do not retroactively affect datasets already delivered to subscribers.

Note

Removal applies to future datasets only. Data already delivered to subscribers before your request is processed will not be recalled.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use by adults and are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us so we can address it.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. This policy does not apply to those websites, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website or services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of the rights described above, contact us and we'll respond promptly.
